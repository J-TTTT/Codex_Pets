# La-Signora — natural gaze mechanics

## Identity and anchor

The canonical front pose has a visible grey-blue CHARACTER RIGHT eye on viewer LEFT. The ornate black mask permanently covers CHARACTER LEFT eye on viewer RIGHT. The mask is rigidly attached to that side of the skull: it follows yaw/pitch and becomes foreshortened, never slides off, switches eyes, becomes a separate floating object, or reveals the hidden eye. Original red/black hair bow remains on its anatomical side. Blonde curls and earrings attach to the head; white fur collar and cape attach to shoulders.

Keep both soles, hips, waist ornament, skirt/cape lower hem, body scale and lower-body center fixed. Only the visible eye, brows/lids, skull/neck and slight upper-chest follow-through express attention. Do not rotate or stretch the whole person. Keep face round and all original feature spacing; no new eyes or eye overlays.

## Natural leading motion

The visible eye leads within its original anime aperture; redraw eye whites, iris, pupil, highlight and eyelid coherently. Follow with restrained head yaw/pitch, approximately 25–35 degrees maximum lateral yaw rather than a full profile. This leaves the visible eye readable while the mask retains believable occlusion. Curls, earrings and mask tips follow the skull smoothly. Collar and cape remain mostly anchored, with only slight shoulder follow-through. No new props or effects.

## Approved cardinal families to establish

- 000 UP: frontal horizontal alignment, chin clearly raised; visible grey-blue iris high beneath upper lid; face plane tilts upward. Must differ clearly from neutral. Mask pitches with face and stays on original side.
- 090 SCREEN RIGHT: nose tip/face plane aim toward the right image edge, visible iris looks right. Head yaws right about 30 degrees; visible-eye cheek turns nearer, while the masked side turns farther and foreshortens without revealing the hidden eye. Nose and visible eye direction must clearly indicate right even if mask partly occludes face.
- 180 DOWN: horizontally centered, chin tucks and visible iris lowers; lowered eyelid/eyebrow and neck angle show down without simply closing eye. Retain a narrow visible iris for directional evidence.
- 270 SCREEN LEFT: nose tip/face plane aim toward left image edge, visible iris looks left. Head yaws left about 30 degrees; mask-bearing cheek turns nearer; mask occupies more of the nearer face while the exposed eye on the farther side may narrow but should remain partly readable at this restrained yaw. Do not mirror the 090 pose; all ornaments retain anatomical sides.

Mask horns are decorations, not pointing features; gaze is established by visible eye plus nose/face plane and chin.

All intermediate poses retain both intended axes. Feet/hem/mask design never jump; earrings/curls follow naturally. Preserve exact source identity, palette, round face and hand-drawn cel style, with all mask tips, cape and footwear inside safe margins.
